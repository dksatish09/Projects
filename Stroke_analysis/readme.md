# Stroke Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze stroke-related health data and identify the major factors associated with stroke occurrence. The analysis compares patients with and without stroke to uncover meaningful health patterns and risk indicators.

## Dataset
Dataset Source: Kaggle
Number of Rows: 5,110
Number of Columns: 12
Domain: Healthcare Analytics

## Tools Used
Language: Python 
        :Jupyter Notebook, 
Libraries: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Visualizations
- Stroke vs Age_group
- Stroke vs gender
- Stroke vs average_glucose_level
- Stroke vs various_factors
- Stroke vs work_type

## Business Insights
Patients with hypertension and heart disease showed a higher probability of stroke occurrence.

Higher glucose levels and increased BMI were strongly associated with stroke risk.

Female patients showed a comparatively higher stroke count in the dataset, mainly influenced by hypertension, obesity, and glucose-related health conditions.

Smoking habits also contributed to increased stroke risk among certain patient groups.

## Recommendations
Reduce consumption of oily and high-salt foods.

Follow a balanced and healthy diet.

Exercise regularly for at least 30–40 minutes daily.

Maintain healthy glucose and blood pressure levels.

Increase water intake and avoid unhealthy fast foods.

Encourage regular health checkups for early detection of risk factors.
